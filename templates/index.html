SENSE OF SELF

You are more than one feeling.

Your sense of self is built from all of your experiences, not only the good ones. Accepting a feeling gives you more room than fighting it.

Every feeling has a job.

Joy, sadness, anger, fear, and the uncomfortable ones too. Each one is information, even when it is hard to sit with.

Protection can take over.

Anxiety tries to keep you safe by planning for what might go wrong. It causes problems when it is the only feeling in charge.

The hard days count too.

Envy, embarrassment, and flat "whatever" days belong in the story of who you are. Some feelings can also wait until you are ready for them.

QUICK RESET

A small way to cope.

When one feeling is doing all the work, a short reset gives the others some room.

Joy

Breathing

Joy does not have to fill the whole day. One slow minute of breath makes room for the other feelings.

Sadness

Journaling

Sadness helps a loss be felt. Write what is here and let it stay on the page.

Anxiety

Step away

Anxiety is trying to protect you by scanning the future. Step away for a few minutes and come back to what is happening now.
